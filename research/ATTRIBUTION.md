# Attribution and scope of included artifacts

Research source code and the EML core are covered by the repository's MIT license. Original measurements, generated arithmetic problems, and figures accompany the code for reproducibility.

Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-0.6B are by the Qwen team. Pinned revisions appear in `PROTOCOL.md` and model-specific protocols. Model-derived directions, feature encoders, and sparse original-neuron control weights originate from these models. Their Apache 2.0 license is included as `licenses/Qwen-Apache-2.0.txt`. The full pretrained weights are not redistributed in this bundle. See the [Qwen3-1.7B model](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B) and [Qwen3-0.6B model](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B).

The Airfoil Self-Noise dataset is by Thomas F. Brooks, D. Stuart Pope, and Michael A. Marcolini (1989), distributed by the UCI Machine Learning Repository under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Dataset DOI: [10.24432/C5VW2C](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VW2C). The original numeric data are included without alteration; split assignments, normalization, a previously trained neural teacher, and the EML surrogate are derived research artifacts. Source: NASA Reference Publication 1218, *Airfoil Self-Noise and Prediction*.

External research cited in the report is prior work, not part of this repository's authorship. This study is not affiliated with or endorsed by Goodfire or the Qwen team.
